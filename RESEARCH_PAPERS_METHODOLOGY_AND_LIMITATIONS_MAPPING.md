### Q8: How does your system implement and improve Paper 9's Cross-Weighted Product Algorithm for technical equipment & invoice search?
> **Answer:** Paper 9 (Zhao et al., IEEE EI2 2020 / CSG Power Generation Company & CIMSTech) addressed the challenge of searching short technical text without natural language syntax (e.g., equipment models, switches, transformer bays). The authors identified that standard Cosine and edit distance fail because in technical codes and serial numbers, the right-side suffix designates the physical type (e.g., searching `"ABC switch"` expects `"ABCD switch"` to rank higher than `"ABC switch cabinet"`). > 1. We implemented the exact mathematical **Cross-Weighted Product Algorithm** in [`app/search/research_algorithms.py`](file:///c:/Users/deshm/Downloads/ocr_image_search_mobile_app/AI_Image_Search/backend/app/search/research_algorithms.py) (`compute_cross_weighted_similarity`):
>    - **Right-Side Positional Weights (Eq. 5):** $W_1[i] = L_1 + i, W_2[i] = L_2 + i$, progressively prioritizing suffix characters.
>    - **Non-Matching Weight Attenuation (Eq. 7):** For characters in $T_2$ absent from $T_1$, weights drop to index $k$, and excess weight $\frac{W_2^{\text{orig}} - k}{L_2 - i}$ is reallocated to downstream characters.
>    - **Continuous Match Range Multiplier (Eq. 8):** For matching characters $T_1[i] == T_2[j]$, bidirectional continuous bounds $[d, u]$ are expanded, multiplying cross-weights: $\sum W_2[j] \cdot \sum_{p=d}^{u} W_1[i+p] / (Sw_1 \cdot Sw_2)$. This heavily rewards contiguous words over scrambled hits (`ABC` vs `ABCD` = $0.692$ vs. `ABC` vs `ABDC` = $0.469$).
> 2. We implemented the **Tri-Part Comprehensive Search Ranking** (Eq. 1 & 2) in `calculate_power_grid_comprehensive_score`:
>    $$S = S_f \times 0.5 + S_s \times 0.3 + S_h \times 0.2 \quad \text{where} \quad S_f = \frac{1}{1 + \exp(-W_s)}$$
> 3. We resolved Paper 9's rigid coefficient limitation by adding **Adaptive Fallback Weighting** for mobile photo search: when structured filter attributes or heat logs are unavailable ($W_s=0$), the system smoothly blends cross-weighted string similarity with OCR visual detection confidence ($S_{\text{attn}}$).

### Q11: How does your system solve the fundamental "Negation Blindness" flaw of SOTA Multimodal AI (Idea 1)?
